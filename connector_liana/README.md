**Turn your Odoo data into automated revenue.**

Connect your CRM to **LianaMailer** and **LianaAutomation** for seamless communication and smarter lead nurturing — no more manual exports, no more outdated lists.

## Why marketing and sales teams choose Liana

Your Odoo database already knows who your customers are and what they do. Liana Technologies turns that knowledge into action. This integration creates a live link between your Odoo operations and your marketing, so every message is timely, relevant, and driven by real data.

- **LianaMailer** — A premium email marketing tool built for high deliverability. Design professional, mobile-responsive newsletters with a drag-and-drop editor, and use the built-in AI Assistant to sharpen your content for maximum impact.
- **LianaAutomation** — Move beyond manual outreach. Build multi-channel customer journeys that react in real time to how your customers engage with your brand.

## What this integration enables

Stop wasting time on CSV exports. Put your Odoo data to work across every campaign.

### 1. Sync your lists for impactful campaigns *(Roadmap Q2/2026)*

Keep your Odoo contacts and LianaMailer lists in perfect harmony.

- **Targeted outreach** — Sync specific segments from Odoo to send personalized campaigns that resonate.
- **Effortless management** — Updates in Odoo are reflected automatically in your mailing lists, so your team always works with the most current data.
- **Advanced analytics** — See exactly how your Odoo contacts engage with your emails through detailed heatmaps and click-through reports.

### 2. Trigger precision automation from your pipeline

Turn Odoo pipeline stages and business processes into automated marketing triggers.

- **Lead nurturing** — Send follow-up content the moment a lead is created or moves to a new stage in Odoo.
- **Onboarding & lifecycle** — Trigger welcome sequences when a deal is closed, or re-engagement flows when a customer goes quiet.
- **Renewals & upsells** — Use Odoo contract dates to trigger automated renewal reminders and targeted offers.
- **Workflow efficiency** — Automate repetitive tasks like meeting follow-ups and webinar invitations based on Odoo event status.

## How to get started

1. **Install** the Liana Integration module.
2. **Connect** your Liana API keys.
   - Get your API keys from Liana Technologies and create a new Backend configuration in Odoo's Liana App.
3. **Map** your Odoo fields and segments.
   - Default mappings for Sales Orders and Leads are provided.
4. **Launch** your first automated journey!
